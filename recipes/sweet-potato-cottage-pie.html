Sweet Potato Cottage Pie

Description

A lighter variant of the classic Cottage Pie recipe, with lean beef mince and sweet potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 450-500g lean beef mince
  • 600g sweet potatoes, peeled and diced into 2-3cm chunks
  • 1 diced brown onion
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • 250ml low-sodium beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
  • 40g mature cheddar, grated
