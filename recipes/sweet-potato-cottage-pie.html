Sweet Potato Cottage Pie

Description

A lighter variant of the classic Cottage Pie recipe, with lean beef mince and sweet potatoes. Access to a pressure fryer and an induction stove is assumed. Serves 3 people.

Ingredients

  • 450-500g lean beef mince
  • 600g sweet potatoes, peeled and diced into ~2-3cm chunks
  • 1 diced brown onion
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • 1 finely diced medium carrot
  • 1 finely diced celery stalk
  • 250ml low-sodium beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
  • 40g mature cheddar, grated

Steps

  1. Steam or pressure cooked sweet potato cubes with 1 cup of water for 6 minutes. Drain thoroughly, and mash until smooth. Season with black pepper and a pinch of salt.
  2. Fry the mince in a pan until brown. Saute onion, garlic, carrot, and celery until softened. Stir in the tomato paste, herbs, Worcestershire sauce, beef stock, and peas. Simmer for 10 minutes until the mix is thickened.
  3. Transfer the filling mixture to the pressure fryer. Spread the mash across the top, rake gently with a fork, and top with the grated cheddar.
  4. Air-fry at 190 degrees Celsius for 8-10 minutes until the edges caramelise and the cheese turns golden.
